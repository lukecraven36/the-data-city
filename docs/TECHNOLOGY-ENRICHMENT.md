## Findings that can support the demonstration

- Five registered offices are in the London region; two in the East of England, one in North West England, one in South West England and one in Scotland. These are administrative locations, not a count of operating sites or local employment.
- Seven companies report SIC 62020 (IT consultancy); five report SIC 62012 (software development). Memberships overlap. These company-level statutory SICs are separate from the Data City R-SIC/RTIC results for our input description.
- The website evidence points to a mixture of software services, consulting and named products. Databotix links to Cheqdin; RR Tech Solutions links to Adam. The input did not produce a verified pure-play proprietary-software cohort.
- Seven latest filings contain previous net-asset comparatives. Show absolute balance-sheet changes with dates, rather than labelling them revenue growth.
- Nine latest filings disclose average employees; the observations range from zero to three. Do not infer that founders, contractors or international teams are absent.
- One latest filing discloses turnover; a second company discloses it only in an older filing. A total-revenue KPI or broad revenue ranking would mislead.
- Five latest filings report negative net assets. Preserve signs, but do not turn this alone into a solvency judgement or an investment score.
